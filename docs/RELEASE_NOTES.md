The launcher now groups your username with a compact **Edit** button in the top-right header, aligned with the rest of the interface.

Download **PokeMulti-Setup.exe**, open the installation wizard, choose a folder,
and launch PokéMulti from the installed shortcut. The versioned ZIP is an
optional portable download and the package used by the launcher updater.

- Edit your online username in the launcher without changing your narrative
  trainer name, identity, friends or progress.
- Check for updates and install future releases from the launcher.
- Windows setup wizard with install-folder selection, shortcuts and uninstaller.
- World saves and trainer identities stay outside the installation and survive
  updates and uninstalling.

Windows 10/11 x64. No ROM included; supply your own supported English FireRed
US 1.0 or 1.1 ROM. Source-code ZIPs are not ready-to-run downloads.
Mac downloads are not available in this release.

Users on 0.25.0 or earlier should install this release once to get the updater.
Experimental, noncommercial build. See the repository's validation and dependency
notices for tested behavior, remaining limitations and credits.
